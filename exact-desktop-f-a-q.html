F.A.Q.

Quick answers to what people ask us most. The binding details on deposits, cancellations and studio rules are in Terms & Conditions.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Sometimes. When an artist has a free slot, we can fit in type of walk-in work on walk-in days and hours. Call us before you come in to check what's possible.

Most of our work is booked ahead, because every piece is planned with your artist. For anything custom, the booking form is the surest way to get a date.

How do I book an appointment?

Start with our booking form. It takes a few minutes and covers your idea, timing, preferred artist, size and placement.

  • You send the form.
  • Our booking team replies by email within reply time, with an artist recommendation, availability and an estimate.
  • You pay the deposit to secure your date.
  • We confirm your appointment and tell you how to prepare.

Sending the form doesn't reserve a slot. Your date is held once your deposit is in. The form doesn't take uploads, so send any reference images when you reply to our message.

How does the deposit work?

Your deposit of deposit amount secures your date and the time your artist spends preparing your design. It's deducted from the final price of your tattoo. You pay it deposit payment method within deposit payment deadline of receiving your estimate. If it isn't paid by then, the slot goes back on the calendar.

If you book online and your appointment is more than 10 business days away, you can cancel within 5 business days of paying your deposit and we'll refund it in full (Mexico's Federal Consumer Protection Law, Art. 56). After that, deposits are non-refundable, except when we cancel and can't offer you a new date.

Your deposit stays with the artist you booked. If you reschedule in time, it moves with you. The full rules are in Terms & Conditions.

How long is the wait for an appointment?

Wait times depend on the design and artist you choose. Smaller pieces usually have a shorter wait, but availability varies with demand and design complexity. Contact us for current wait times.

Guest artists are with us on set dates, so their slots tend to fill first. If your dates are flexible, mention it in your description. It gives us more room to find you a spot.

Can I get tattooed while I'm visiting Mexico City?

Yes. In the booking form, add your travel dates to your description. We'll suggest an artist who's free while you're here and settle the estimate and deposit before you arrive. We work in languages spoken, from your first message to your session.

Book as far ahead as you can. If your trip includes the beach, schedule your session near the end of your stay: a fresh tattoo has to stay out of the sea, pools and strong sun until it heals, and we can't tattoo sunburned skin. Touch-ups need a healed tattoo, so ask us how they work if you won't be back in Mexico City soon.

Which artist is right for me?

Start with the artist profiles. Each one shows what that artist specializes in, so look for the work that feels closest to your idea.

Still not sure? Choose "Recommend me" in the booking form. Our booking team will match you by style, size, budget and dates, and tell you why.

We also host guest artists on set dates and announce their visits on our Instagram, @nomadttt. To book one, choose "Guest Artists" in the form and write their name or Instagram in the field that appears. Their dates are fixed, so slots fill quickly and there's less room to reschedule.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes, when the tattoo you have now allows it. Its size, darkness and age decide what's possible, so we assess every request before saying yes. Cover-ups are done by artists who do cover-ups.

Mention it in your booking form description. When we reply, send clear, well-lit photos of the tattoo you have now.

Expect the new piece to be larger and darker than the old one. Sometimes a few laser sessions to lighten it first open up better options.

How much does a tattoo cost?

It depends on size, placement, detail, color and the time it takes. Rates are set how rates are set, and guest artists may price differently.

Our studio minimum is minimum price. Once we've reviewed your request, we send you an estimate in Mexican pesos (MXN). If you have a price range in mind, add the figure to your description in the booking form. It helps us suggest the right artist and approach.

Your deposit is deducted from the final price. If the size, placement or design changes on the day, the price can change with it.

How old do I need to be?

You must be minimum age or older to get tattooed with us. Bring a valid photo ID to every appointment: a passport, a driver's license or a Mexican INE voter card. Mexican health rules require us to record it.

Without valid ID we can't tattoo you, and you lose your deposit.

Needles and cartridges are single-use and opened in front of you, and reusable tools are sterilized between clients. health permits held

Before your session you fill in a short health questionnaire and sign a consent letter, as Mexican health rules require. That's where you share any health details, in private.

How should I prepare for my tattoo?

A rested, well-fed body handles a session better. Before you come in:

  • Sleep well and eat a proper meal a few hours before.
  • Skip alcohol for at least 24 hours. It thins the blood.
  • Keep the area moisturized and out of the sun. We can't tattoo sunburned or irritated skin.
  • Wear comfortable clothes that give easy access to the placement.
  • Bring water, a snack for longer sessions, and a valid photo ID.
  • Ask your artist before using numbing cream.

If you have a health condition or allergy, take regular medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, just tell us when you book that there's something health-related to check. Don't include details: you'll share them privately in the health questionnaire before your session. Feeling unwell? Let us know early and we'll find a new date.

Yes, you can bring number of companions allowed. Let us know when you book so we can plan the space.

Whether they sit with you during the session is up to your artist: some setups are tight, and some pieces need full focus. For hygiene and safety, please leave children and pets at home.

You'll always see it before anything touches your skin. Your artist shares the design when designs are shared, and you review it together. Small adjustments to size and placement are part of the process, and a stencil lets you check the placement on your body first.

You don't need a finished design to book. Choose "No, I need help & guidance" in the booking form, and your artist will develop it with you. Have a drawing or references? Describe them in the form and send them when we reply. Your artist uses them as a starting point and adapts them to you, without copying another artist's work.

If you want to change the idea itself, tell us at least design change notice before your appointment. Bigger changes can affect the time, the price and sometimes the date.

How do I care for my new tattoo?

At the end of your session, your artist explains how to look after your tattoo. Their instructions come first. As a general guide:

  • Wash it gently with clean hands and fragrance-free soap, then pat it dry.
  • Apply a thin layer of fragrance-free moisturizer a few times a day.
  • Let it peel on its own. Don't pick or scratch.
  • Stay out of pools, the sea, baths and saunas until it has healed.
  • Keep it out of direct sun while it heals, and use a high SPF once it has.

The surface usually heals in two to four weeks; the skin underneath takes longer. Redness, swelling or heat that gets worse after the first few days isn't normal. See a doctor and let us know.

Yes. If any area needs it once your tattoo has fully healed, you get one free touch-up within touch-up period of your session. It's done by your original artist, or by another resident artist if they're no longer with us. Send us clear photos of the healed tattoo first so we can check it.

The free touch-up doesn't cover areas excluded from free touch-ups, design changes, or fading caused by sun, skipped aftercare or skin changes. After that period, touch-ups are quoted separately. For guest pieces, it depends on the artist's next visit.
